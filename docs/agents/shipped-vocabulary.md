# Shipped vocabulary

Moved from [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), which points here. The shipped names and what each Slice 1 step shipped. For new work, the specification's glossary wins. These rules are binding when a task touches this area.

Use these terms consistently in code, migrations, UI labels, tests, and documentation.

The table and paragraph below describe shipped vocabulary. Slice 1 step 1 applied ADR 0016 to the Office and Current office rows and ADR 0017 to the Agency user row. The M3 and M4 vocabulary was deleted with those layers in Phase 1. For new work, the specification's glossary wins.

| Term | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| Agency | The travel agency using DepartureDesk and owning operational records. Agency is the tenant. Status is `active`, `suspended`, or `closed`. |
| Workspace code | The immutable, globally unique sign-in key for an agency. Stored already normalized. Request `agency_id` never establishes tenancy. |
| Agency time zone | `Agency.default_timezone`, the one IANA zone for the agency. Offices carry none (ADR 0016). |
| Office | An optional agency-owned record for assignment, filtering and reporting. It is not an authorization grant and not a tenant. An agency may have none. Do not use `Branch`. |
| Agency user | An account that belongs to exactly one agency. The same email in two agencies is two accounts. Its user type is exactly `agency_admin`, `staff` or `reseller` (ADR 0017). |
| Permission | One of five per-user grants a staff user may hold: `departure_management`, `supplier_arrangements`, `financial_records`, `passport_access`, `reseller_relationships`. An Agency Admin holds all five without stored grants. |
| Reseller | A reseller relationship, which is also its one channel (D-68). A `reseller` user belongs to exactly one. Since Slice 1 step 9a, staff authorize one and set its direct-contact setting (ACC-13, DOC-08); every relationship is Active in Slice 1. Since step 9b, staff invite its users, and a reseller user works only in the Reseller namespace (`/reseller`), through `ResellerScope`. |
| Relationship | A descriptive string on an agency user. It does not grant access. |
| Session | Authentication root. It derives its agency through the agency user and does not store `agency_id`. |
| Current office | A session preference, then the user's default office, then nil. Changing it changes no permission. |

Client Person, Client Organization, Client (person-backed or organization-backed), person-owned and organization-owned contact points, organization-contact assignments, Supplier, Supplier categories, Supplier-owned contact points, Supplier Location, Supplier Contact, and Supplier Contact-owned email and phone destinations are shipped vocabulary. Since Slice 1 step 2, a Departure has three statuses (sales status Draft or Ready to sell, operating decision, completion status) and a derived summary label (DEP-03, DEP-04), a reference issued at creation (D-91), an append-only decision history, a Departure team with an optional default task owner (ACC-09, OPS-12), and optional hold-duration and installment-buffer overrides. Activation and the schedule, currency and lifecycle corrections were retired in that step. Since Slice 1 step 3a, a supplier has payee accounts (SUP-02), and a Departure has supplier arrangements (Draft, Confirmed, Closed, Cancelled; SUP-11) with documents, inventory groups (INV-01 to INV-04), capacity rules (INV-39), a supplier rate table (SUP-35) frozen at confirmation, entered as a grid: a pricing pattern of bands (positions, optionally for one traveler type), values every fare shares, and per-fare overrides, saved as one rate line per position, and, from confirmation, unit shells and capacity pools on an append-only capacity ledger (ADR 0018). Since step 3b, an arrangement has supplier deadlines (SUP-22 to SUP-24) that keep their local date, time and zone and a stored resolved instant (SET-10), and an actionable deadline has a task: one record per task, with an individual owner, a status (Open, In progress, Completed, Waived) and overdue derived from the clock (OPS-02, OPS-03). Staff land on My tasks after sign-in. Since step 4, an arrangement has cost lines (estimated or committed; a commitment for unsold space names its shells, D-75), schedule lines with SMN-04's due rules, append-only trigger events (SMN-11) and append-only amounts due, posted when a schedule line's date arrives or its event is recorded; a rate line records what its commission rate applies to. Since step 5, a Departure has package offerings whose contents are choices (included or add-on; per traveler, unit or trip; required single, optional single or optional multiple) with options drawing on its inventory groups and client prices by occupancy position and traveler type (BKG-13 to BKG-16, BKG-21); and numbered client terms versions with the agency's copied wording, the Departure's own, cancellation tiers (the agency defaults unless overridden, BKG-31) and a payment schedule template (CMN-09), locked once a booking accepts one. Since step 6a, a traveler profile is one person with a legal name, date of birth and contacts but no passport fields (CLT-05, CLT-28), linked to clients by a relationship that grants nothing (CLT-09, CLT-10), and a Client Trip has one responsible client, at most one Departure and a reference issued at creation (CLT-02, OVR-02, D-94). Since step 6b, a Client Trip has client bookings (Draft, Confirmed, Discarded or Cancelled; BKG-05, BKG-06) of its Departure's package, created only while the Departure is On sale (DEP-05); a booking has travelers or placeholders with a traveler type (CLT-13, CLT-15), choice selections whose per-unit units have occupants with positions in that unit, booking price lines proposed once and kept (BKG-23), and inventory allocations (Held, Reserved or Released, forward only) that a lapsed hold stops consuming at read time (D-88). Shell status derives Partly and Fully allocated (INV-38); an agency reduction below consuming allocations is refused (INV-26), and a supplier release below them leaves a derived capacity conflict. Traveler types may count as adults, and a group may require adults per unit (INV-09). Since step 6c, a booking records the client's acceptance (BKG-41), append-only and stale once the price lines or terms change (BKG-39); the first acceptance locks the terms version and copies the agency tiers it followed. Confirmation (BKG-45) reserves the held space, snapshots names as booked, makes the client charge, proposes booking-scoped supplier cost (none for shells already committed as unsold space, D-75) and expected commission, and records a Client booking confirmed event naming the booking's shells, so a per-shell line posts only for them (D-81). Since step 7, a supplier reservation is its own record (INV-33): a request for a booking's units from one arrangement at its scope (SUP-10), Requested then Confirmed with the supplier's reference (INV-34, INV-35, REF-09), with append-only dated events; on-request, rate-only and directly booked units need one, block units under arrangement scope don't, and a confirmed booking's Requested unit shows Awaiting supplier (INV-37). Decline and cancel are Slice 1b. Since step 8a, a draft booking has proposed installments from its terms' payment schedule, adjustable before acceptance, which covers them (CMN-09); client payments, their applications, reversals and receipts belong to the Client Trip, and a designated-payment acceptance names its payment (BKG-41). Since step 9a, an offering is published to a reseller's channel as an offering publication, open until withdrawn by staff or by a return to Draft (DEP-07 to DEP-09); a booking has an owner and a seller of record, each the agency or a reseller, apart from its creator (ACC-18, FIN-05); and a client or traveler records who created it (D-78). Since step 9b, a reseller user sees the offers published to its relationship and the bookings it owns (R-01 to R-03; ACC-14); it creates a booking's client, travelers and trip on a published offer, with no duplicate check, and the booking is owned by its relationship with it as seller of record (D-99 (1), CLT-25); it holds, records acceptance, confirms at the offering's current prices and terms only (DEP-10), and discards while the hold is active (BKG-56); it sees charges, installments, payments and balance but records no money (CMN-19, CMN-52).
