# Money

Moved from [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), which points here. Client and supplier money as shipped. These rules are binding when a task touches this area.

Since Slice 1 step 4, supplier money has cost lines, schedule lines and amounts due (SMN-01), in integer minor units of the arrangement currency; an amount due is snapshotted when posted and never recalculated, and posted amounts and trigger events are append-only. Since step 6c, booking confirmation makes the client charge and its charge lines (CMN-01 to CMN-03), never edited, and proposes the booking's supplier cost and expected commission from the rate table (SUP-35, SMN-32), kept apart. Since step 8a, a confirmed booking's charge has installments made from the accepted proposals (CMN-08, CMN-12); client payments are recorded on the trip by route with a `PAY-` reference, applied to installments with the rest held as credit, reversed rather than edited, and an agency payment issues a gap-free `RCP-` receipt, while a supplier-route payment is never agency cash (CMN-18 to CMN-29, CMN-55); all are append-only. Since step 8b, supplier payments carry an `SPY-` reference and are applied to that supplier's amounts due on the Departure, the rest left unapplied, reversed rather than edited, and never change cost or capacity (SMN-18 to SMN-23); an amount due is Unpaid, Part paid or Paid; a schedule line's payment task completes as the system once settled; the arrangement balance basis posts committed cost less what other lines made due (D-96); expected commission can be confirmed (SMN-33). When later steps add client charges, payments and commission, keep those facts distinct from supplier obligations, store integer minor units, and do not persist functional-currency translations until ADR 0001 is amended. `money-rails` is a value-object layer only.
