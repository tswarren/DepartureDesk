## Recorded gaps

None of the six fixtures prove:

- Air or rail.
- Travel insurance.
- A guide with no controlled capacity.
- Externally managed inventory.
- Multiple currencies.
- A Supplier change after activation.

ABC Motorcoach states an on-request ceiling of two additional coaches. That is partial coverage of on-request services, not a separate fixture.

Variable nightly Hotel blocks are proved by Hilton Fort Lauderdale Marina. They are not a gap.
