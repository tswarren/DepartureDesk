# Wine Country Tour — Canonical Supplier Composition Fixture

## 1. Status and authority

**Status:** Draft. Not implementation authority.

This fixture supersedes earlier Wine Country Tour facts where they conflict. No slice may rely on it until it is Approved and an accepted slice plan names it.

## 2. Purpose

Prove that one Departure can coordinate five independent Supplier Arrangements without collapsing them into a mixed-DMC agreement. Each Supplier retains its own service identity, capacity, costs, deposits, deadlines, and cancellation exposure.

## 3. Source classification

Illustrative Supplier Composition facts. Personal expenses and gratuities for the tour director are excluded from the Supplier cost. No unresolved fact blocks the stated proof.

## 9. Cancellation terms

Each Supplier uses the same ladder structure but retains its own base and service-date boundaries:

1. Any required deposit becomes nonrefundable when due.
2. At 30 days before the Supplier's first service, the cumulative cancellation charge becomes 50% of that Supplier's guaranteed minimum commitment.
3. At 14 days before the first service, it becomes 75%.
4. At seven days before the first service, it becomes 100%.
5. Deposits are credited toward the cumulative cancellation charge; they are not added on top of it.

| Supplier | Cancellation base | 50% from | 75% from | 100% from |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Grand Traverse Hotel | $6,600 | Apr 21 | May 7 | May 14 |
| Harbor Table | $2,125 | Apr 21 | May 7 | May 14 |
| North Star Tour Directors | $1,350 | Apr 21 | May 7 | May 14 |
| Cherry Capital Coaches | $1,400 | Apr 22 | May 8 | May 15 |
| Old Mission Cellars | $1,300 | Apr 22 | May 8 | May 15 |

The coach and tour-director ladders are anchored to their first actual service dates. The coach is not treated as a three-day service.

## 11. Required proof

This fixture should prove that DepartureDesk can:

- coordinate five independent Supplier Arrangements under one Departure;
- preserve one or more exact Arrangement Items and Occurrences per Supplier;
- distinguish controlled capacity from planning quantities;
- represent fixed, per-person, per-day, per-room, and complimentary Supplier-cost shapes;
- distinguish contractual minimum exposure from a 30-traveler planning forecast;
- scope percentage deposits to explicit Supplier-cost contributors;
- keep each deposit, balance deadline, final-count deadline, and cancellation ladder Supplier-specific;
- credit deposits against cumulative cancellation exposure; and
- reopen every fact by stable identity rather than label or creation order.

## 12. Explicit exclusions

This fixture does not decide:

- how the five Supplier services are packaged or sold;
- whether any service is included or optional;
- Client prices, margins, discounts, deposits, or payment schedules;
- Client choices or rate-category keys;
- traveler reservations or bookings;
- Supplier Payments, invoices, or paid status; or
- a shared DMC-level deposit, deadline, or cancellation policy.

Client pricing, Package construction, and Client Trip selection belong to their own layers.
