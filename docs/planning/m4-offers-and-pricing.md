## Goal and boundary

Describe what an Agency intends to sell on one Departure as versioned Packages and independently sellable services. A Package is optional: it composes exact service-definition versions, states inclusion and choice rules, and may carry a bundled price or a service-sum price. M4 makes offer price, terms, supply basis, and indicative margin explainable for anonymous booking scenarios. Publication means eligible for future internal M5 selection; it is not a public storefront or a Client sale.

M4 creates no Client Trip, Traveler Assignment, named occupancy, Hold, Allocation, Supplier booking for a Client, Charge, Receipt, Supplier Obligation, Payment, earned commission, cancellation case, or Communication. M3 Supplier arrangements and cost forecasts remain independent of Client prices. Travel Program, FX, automated reminders, arbitrary formula scripting, and service-specific subclasses remain out of scope.

## Record and lifecycle contract

[ADR 0014](../adr/0014-client-offers-publication-and-supply-compatibility.md) owns identity, publication, source compatibility, Sales enabled versus computed eligibility, and M5 handoff. An accepted M4A plan still names persistence tables before migrations.

- An Agency/Departure-owned stable Service Offer identity has immutable numbered versions; versions own client-visible service definitions, pricing, terms, choice-group templates, and fulfillment bindings. Staff may draft a package-only service in the Package builder and publish both exact versions in **one command**. Only standalone or deliberately reusable services need a separate publication task. Package-only visibility does not pretend that an included service was independently offered. **Service Offer** is M4 vocabulary, distinct from Arrangement Item and from later Client Trip Service.
- Choice groups on a Service Offer version are templates for the eventual Client Trip Service. M5 must snapshot the exact selected group rules, alternatives, selections, and price/terms provenance into its service context whether the offer was standalone or included in a Package. A Package is not required for a choice group to apply.
- An Agency/Departure-owned stable Package identity has immutable numbered versions. An exact Package version pins exact Service Offer versions and owns inclusion, choice placements, package price method, optional price adjustments, sales limit/window, and terms. One service version can be reused across Packages without changing it. A package successor copies a draft graph; rejecting it leaves the published version intact.
- Publication captures a manifest of exact service and Supplier source versions, price-rule/term fingerprints, actor, and timestamp. Draft graphs can change under optimistic locking; published definition rows and the manifest are immutable in Rails and PostgreSQL. Pause/resume/retire are explicit operational events rather than edits to the published definition. Audit `details` are not the manifest store; extend `AuditEvent` action and subject catalogs in the same change that first writes them.
- Offer drafts may exist on a draft or active Departure and may be prepared against current M3 planning context. **Publication requires an active Departure.** **Publication of an M3-backed binding requires activated exact-version source definitions** (Item, Occurrence, Resource, and/or Pool as bound). A draft Arrangement version remains editable and is not a publication pin. A successor **draft** does not govern sale eligibility; only the current activated version, or a later activated successor proven compatible, may. Agency-fulfilled and externally fulfilled services have no Arrangement pin. A stored IANA zone is required when a sales window is set.
- A Supplier Arrangement successor does **not** automatically invalidate all offers that reference its predecessor. Compare only the exact Item/Occurrence/Resource/Pool definitions and Supplier terms that the offer binds or exposes, not the whole Arrangement graph or its version number. `copied_from` proves lineage, not equality. Equivalent bound definitions preserve eligibility and published provenance; a compatibility result identifies the current activated source for M5 selection. A materially changed or unprovable bound dependency blocks that selection path until reviewed and republished. A cost-only difference refreshes the indicative economics and shows a derived notice; a numeric Pool quantity change belongs in live availability. [ADR 0014](../adr/0014-client-offers-publication-and-supply-compatibility.md) defines this narrow comparison and how M5 records both published provenance and the current compatible source. No published definition is silently rebound.
- Lifecycle eligibility is an M4 contract, not an inferred M3 side effect. A departed Departure blocks new publication and forward selection. For an M3-backed path, ended Arrangements, cancelled Occurrences, and inactive bound Suppliers make the affected binding ineligible. Every required binding must be eligible; an alternative group remains usable when another valid member can be selected. Live preview discloses the unavailable path and reason. Arrangement ending and Supplier inactivation never rewrite published offers; a materially changed required dependency calls for reviewed republication, while an unused invalid alternative does not disable a viable path.
- Offer review state is derived from the published manifest and current activated M3 source by default. Record consequential Staff acknowledgments or decisions durably, but add no stored M4 review projection or rebuild hooks unless measured read performance justifies them in an accepted slice. Do not expand the shipped M3E.5 closed Supplier Needs-attention detector catalog for Client-offer concerns.
- Client prices use the Departure operating currency. [ADR 0011](../adr/0011-supplier-cost-definitions-and-forecast-evaluation.md) already requires every Supplier cost definition, including both stages of a source, to use that same currency. There is no distinct-stage FX path in M3. A currency mismatch fails closed. Published Client price records extend the existing currency-bearing freeze on `UpdateDeparture` / `CorrectDepartureCurrency`. Cross-currency margin is unknown unless a later accepted conversion contract exists.
- Scenario cost and margin evaluation uses the scenario's quantities in memory. It must not persist scenario demand as M3C usage assumptions or occupancy profiles.
- A bundled Package may define the narrow Vineyard-style single-occupancy variant. Its selected value is a Package price source, never a second simultaneous service/resource occupancy charge for the same supplement. Define the variant's exact base, quantity, calculation order, and rounding in M4B. The MVP occupancy exception is accepted.
- Sale readiness is distinct from a numeric capacity promise. Publication enforces valid structure, exact same-tenant **activated** sources where M3-backed, computable price for declared examples, applicable currency, **structurally** valid choice rules, and truthful fulfillment labels. A temporary capacity shortage belongs in the live preview, not the publication gate. On-request and external sources remain visibly unconfirmed; the UI must not present them as guaranteed inventory.
- Published terms include recorded percentage bases, calculation order, included/additive treatment, rounding policy, currency, relative or fixed due milestones, and eligibility. The Package provides the bundled Client payment schedule by default. Staff add service-specific conditions when they apply; inherited terms appear together at review, and only actual conflicts need an explicit resolution. Do not invent a Client deposit or cancellation rule from an M3 Supplier term. M5 confirmation revalidates and snapshots Client terms and Supplier provenance; future installments remain scheduled under the accepted commercial contract.
- A Client cancellation policy can combine computable ordered tiers with explicitly manual-review clauses. Its M4 preview explains the recorded terms and whether an example consequence is calculable; it posts no cancellation consequence and must never interpret a manual clause as zero liability.
- An alternative source is an explicit scenario choice. A price/cost/capacity preview counts every required source and only the chosen eligible member of each alternative group. Invalid unused alternatives remain visible with reasons. Package and included service sales limits are cumulative constraints, not substitutes for each other or M3 capacity.
- A service successor alone does not invalidate a published Package pinned to an eligible prior version. Retirement of the pinned version or a material change to its bound Supplier source does. Package and service Client terms are scoped; conflicts require an explicit versioned resolution before publication, with the original service terms still traceable.

## Staff task flow and friction budget

An ordinary Supplier-backed offer should take four conceptual steps, without forcing Staff through an independent service catalog or a general formula builder:

1. **Add from Supplier planning.** Pick an Item or Occurrence; prefill source, provider, category, dates, currency, known capacity, and relevant cost context. An on-request/Agency/external service instead starts from an explicit fulfillment choice. Drafting may begin before Arrangement activation and on a draft Departure; publication of an M3-backed pin waits for the activated definition and an active Departure.
2. **Price the service.** Start with a suitable fixed, per-person, per-resource/night, or occupancy pattern; show the result in Client terms. Expose component ordering, alternative source groups, and special rates only when needed.
3. **Optionally make a Package.** Add included and optional services, define any choice, select bundled or service-sum price, and add one Package-wide payment schedule. Package-only services publish with the Package; separately sellable services have their own path.
4. **Review and publish once.** One combined review shows Client price, choices, the staff's Sales enabled setting, calculated current eligibility with reasons, dates, applicable terms, and optional indicative margin. Staff can address hard structural errors in context. Live capacity does not become a publication prerequisite.

The default UI has one plain **Sales enabled** control. Its value expresses Staff intent; a separate computed label shows **Selectable now**, **On request**, or **Unavailable**, with the current reason. Pause/resume and retirement remain distinct audited events behind that surface. Optional sales caps, multiple Supplier sources, complex percentage bases, and cost breakdown stay in progressive disclosures. Every slice must extend this journey without duplicating data entry or requiring a second publication click for a package-only service. [M4.0](m40-task-flow-and-contract.md) records the friction contract; M4E measures against it rather than inventing counts here.

## Invariants and proof obligations

1. Derive Agency from the session and load Departure, M3 source, service, and Package through that tenant. Database constraints must reject cross-Agency and cross-Departure links; unauthorized IDs return not found. Office context is not a permission grant.
2. Version numbers and publication are unique and atomic under concurrent requests. Consequential commands use durable idempotency and preserve same-key replay semantics; expected failures create neither half a published manifest nor an audit success event.
3. A published graph and its exact source references cannot be edited in place, including child rows. Successor drafts may be discarded without changing their published predecessor. Preserve lifecycle history and actor/reason.
4. Publication of an M3-backed binding validates **activated** exact-source integrity and choice structure on an **active** Departure. Staff Sales enabled state is distinct from computed eligibility. Live preview reads current Departure and per-path source lifecycle, sales date, caps, choices, and scenario quantities, labels its time/snapshot, and explains Selectable now, On request, or Unavailable. Required bindings all apply; an alternative group needs one selected usable member. The preview is advisory and may become stale. M5's eventual Hold/selection/confirmation commands must use an authoritative transactional recheck under a documented lock order. Neither M4 publication nor preview reserves capacity.
5. Use Departure operating currency for Client prices under the accepted MVP no-FX boundary. M3 cost definitions, including both stages of a source, already use that same currency. A mismatch fails closed. Published Client prices extend the currency-bearing freeze. Cross-currency margin is unknown unless an accepted conversion contract exists. Percentage components explicitly select bases, ordering, included/additive treatment, and line rounding. No NaN, implicit zero cost, or fabricated commission receipt.
6. A service-sum Package preserves component prices and named adjustments; a bundled Package preserves one bundled price source without attributing it among suppliers. A choice preview counts only selected options. M5 must snapshot the exact Service Offer choice-group template and selected alternatives on its Client Trip Service whether the offer is standalone or part of a Package. Client Trip confirmation in M5 will snapshot selected offer versions and post due Charges atomically with allocation and reference issuance.
7. Staff may publish structurally complete offers even if temporary capacity is exhausted or cost is unknown, with accurate live disclosure. Tenant, currency, and version-integrity failures cannot be bypassed. There is no Administrator publication override in the initial contract. Viewer reads Client-facing published facts; indicative margin is Staff/Administrator only, per [M4.0](m40-task-flow-and-contract.md).
8. System and UI tests exercise exact-version pinning, activated-only publication pins, equivalent versus material bound-source successors, package-only joint publication, Sales enabled versus computed state, temporary shortage without publication blockage, viable alternatives beside inactive or cancelled paths, fulfillment-label versus Pool-mode disclosure, standalone and Package choice-template handoff, local sales-window boundary, stacked limit bases, percentage/rounding, unknown and enrollment-dependent margin, 375/768/1280/1400 layouts, keyboard paths, and Viewer-safe branches. Test M3 regressions and full Docker/CI suite at the release gate.

## Slice-owned decisions

1. **Price-rule detail for M4B:** Specify a bounded matrix of quantity units, Client rate categories, occupancy positions, dates, rounding, and tax-inclusive displays, plus which simple UI pattern exposes each. Do not copy Supplier cost rules wholesale or require an advanced editor for every offer. Include the shared fixed-cost enrollment/margin case.
2. **Alternative source topology for M4A:** Define allowed required/alternative group shapes and M5 handoff without double-counting. Required bindings must all be eligible; an alternative group uses one explicitly selected eligible member. An invalid unused alternative does not block another viable path. Keep the one-source flow default. Translate the [ADR 0014](../adr/0014-client-offers-publication-and-supply-compatibility.md) field boundary into binding-specific normalized comparisons and tests.
3. **Offer-limit accounting for M4C/M5:** Specify counted quantities and future M5 consumption under optional stacked Package/service limits, choices, and nonnumeric on-request/external sources. Do not require a separate cap when Supplier capacity is the only constraint.
4. **Term resolution for M4C:** Define a source-traceable Package-version resolution only for an actual scoped conflict. Silent precedence is disallowed, but ordinary included services should require no resolution form.

## Closed by ADR 0014 and M4.0

- Source compatibility compares only bound or exposed facts. `copied_from` is lineage only. Material or unknown comparisons block the affected selection path; mere version increment, cost-only changes, and numeric Pool quantity changes do not by themselves force republication.
- M4 issues no generated Package or Service Offer references. Staff identify records by Departure, name, and version; durable links use UUIDs. A later slice that needs generated references must amend [ADR 0004](../adr/0004-human-readable-references.md) first.
- There is no Administrator Client-offer override in the initial contract. Never reuse `override_supplier_planning_terms`.
- Documentation base is `ea6d63e`. Coding slices reconfirm CI on the branch tip.

## Parent exit criteria

M4 is complete when Staff can create an ordinary Supplier-backed offer through the four-step path without duplicate source entry or a second package-only service publication; preview, publish, pause/resume, and supersede Packages and standalone services; and reach advanced pricing/source controls only when required. Sales enabled is visibly distinct from computed eligibility. Exact Supplier/service provenance, activated-only publication pins, same-currency Client prices, per-path lifecycle eligibility, and conservative bound-source comparison are proven without requiring a persisted review projection by default. Celebrity and Vineyard explain prices, standalone and Package choices, current capacity/uncertainty, and optional indicative margin without inventing unresolved terms; Vineyard's 100% supplement remains labeled illustrative; shared fixed-cost margin is either enrollment-labeled scenario economics or unknown. M5 receives exact selected version, choice, and term information. Tenancy, permissions, concurrency, immutable history, rounding, accessibility, performance, integrated CI, and the revised roadmap/MVP handoff are proven. No M5 or M6 domain records are implied by this gate.
