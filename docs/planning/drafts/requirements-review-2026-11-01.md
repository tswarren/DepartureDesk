## Additional planning issues exposed by the review

1. **Phase 0 readiness is overstated.** The planning index says every data-model decision is logged and only writing/reviewing the model and screens remains. Items 1, 2, 6, 7 and 9 above still affect that design; item 3 requires an explicit path choice. Update the standing after these findings are triaged. No build work starts on the strength of D-61 to D-73 alone.
2. **Hold expiry crosses a declared scope boundary.** Workflow step 10 includes BKG-10, while notifications are placed in later slices. Decide the narrow notification needed for that accepted behavior and amend the charter if necessary; do not infer permission for the full subsystem. “Step 10” in the supplied list means workflow step 10, which is build step 6.
3. **D1 closes before the scheduled cost build.** Build step 3 closes D1 with committed cost recorded, but step 4 is supplier cost/schedule construction. Decide whether minimal committed cost persistence belongs in step 3 or D1 closes in step 4. A test cannot claim D1 is complete from cabin creation alone.
4. **Completion and reconciliation need one consistent representation.** DEP's completion lifecycle transitions to Financially reconciled, while DEP-43/DEP-45 and D-60 preserve operational Departed and the reopening scenario retains it. Clarify whether reconciliation is separate from operational completion, or how the original terminal state remains available and drives labels. Do not invent a fourth status or collapse the history during model design.
