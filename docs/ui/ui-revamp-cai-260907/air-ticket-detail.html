Agency-settled vs. supplier-collected

Agency-settled — both tickets on this PNR

  • Agency collects money from the client
  • Agency owes the reported ARC/BSP settlement amount
  • Commission and service fees contribute to agency revenue
  • Settlement amount appears as an agency obligation

Supplier-collected — not used here

  • The airline charges the client directly
  • Recorded as client trip cost, not agency-held cash
  • Agency may still earn commission or a separate service fee
  • No agency settlement obligation unless reported otherwise
This distinction is why "collected by" appears as its own column above rather than being inferred from who the ticket is billed to.
